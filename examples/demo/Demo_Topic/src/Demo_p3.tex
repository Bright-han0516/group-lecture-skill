\jtprac{}{Practice: free fall}
\jtexamtip{Common patterns: choose a sign convention, then apply $s=\tfrac12gt^2$ from rest.}

\jtselfq{W2}{2}{A ball is dropped from rest. Calculate the time it takes to fall 20 m. Ignore air resistance.}
\jtsolution{中文标准答案：取向下为正，$u=0$，$s=20$ m，$a=g=9.81$ m s$^{-2}$。由 $s=\tfrac12gt^2$ 得 $t=\sqrt{2s/g}=\sqrt{40/9.81}\approx 2.0$ s。}
